Passato Prossimo

Conjugate the given verb to fill in the blanks with the appropriate form of the Passato Prossimo
Ex: (parlare) La settimana scorsa ho parlato con Luigi.
The auxiliary may be essere or avere, depending on the verb.
Pay attention to agreements and irregular past participles!
